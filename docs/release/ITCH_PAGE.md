**Title:** RayShot

**Tagline:** A tactical aim trainer powered by my own ray-sphere math.

## Description

RayShot is a first-person aim trainer for Windows. Four aim modes train flicking, precision, tracking and reaction time, and a Training Center lets you practise with five weapons against bots.

Every shot is decided by a ray-sphere intersection ported from my own C ray tracer: solve the quadratic, check the discriminant, take the nearest hit. No Unreal line traces involved.

After each session, the Flick Optimizer shows your accuracy per direction and suggests a sensitivity, and an offline AI Coach gives three specific tips. It is rule based and needs no internet. Your bests and history are saved locally.

## Features

- 4 aim modes: Gridshot, Microshot, Tracking, Reflex
- 5 weapons with recoil, spread and ADS
- Tactical movement
- Training Center with Static, Strafing, Peek and Flick bots
- Flick Optimizer and offline AI Coach
- Personal bests and saved settings
- Cyberpunk UI, 4 colour themes

## Controls

WASD move, mouse look, Space jump, Shift walk, Ctrl crouch, LMB fire, RMB aim, R reload, 1-5 weapons, T Training Center, M Training Center menu, Tab back/pause.

## Install

1. Download and unzip `RayShot-v1.0-Windows.zip`.
2. Run `RayShot.exe`.
3. If Windows SmartScreen warns, click More info -> Run anyway (the build is unsigned).

Requires Windows 10/11 64-bit and a DirectX 12 GPU.
